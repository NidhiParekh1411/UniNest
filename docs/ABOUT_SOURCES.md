Every institutional claim rendered by `web/src/pages/About.jsx` and
`web/src/lib/site.js` comes from one of the pages below, checked September 2026.
Nothing on that page is invented. If you edit the copy, keep it that way — a
project that fabricates facts about a real university is a bad look, and it is
the kind of thing an examiner will spot.

## One correction worth knowing

An early draft of this work assumed GLS was founded in **1935**. It was not —
that year belongs to the **Ahmedabad Education Society**, a different
institution. The Gujarat Law Society was founded in **1927**. If you have used
1935 anywhere in a report or slide deck, fix it.

## The disclaimer on the site

The About page ends with a note stating that this is a student project, not an
official university website, that the institutional facts come from the sources
above, and that the student records inside the app are generated sample data.
**Leave that paragraph in.** It is the thing that makes using a real
university's name here unambiguously fine.
